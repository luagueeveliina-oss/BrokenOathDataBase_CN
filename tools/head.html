How to read this: attacks show damage as a % of your weapon's damage, spells show their base damage roll, before gear, attributes and passives. Each row is the gem's own level. Levels from gear and passives ("Plays at level X") work differently: spells grow 19% per level either way, attacks get 6% more damage per gear level instead of their normal per-level gain. Gems max at 20, so rows 21-40 assume a level 20 gem plus gear levels. Worked out from the game's own damage code and checked against an in-game tooltip.
Pulled from the Broken Oath Playtest data files, updated 9 November 2026 patch (build 65). Playtest numbers will change between builds. Ranges like (30-45) are the roll range; affix ranges span every tier. DPS on weapons is physical damage × attacks per second at the best rolls, before crit, gems and passives.
